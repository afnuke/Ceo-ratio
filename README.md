# CEO Ratio

Ground Truths, app #2 — **owner vs. employee pay.**

GroundTruth (app #1) shows you a company's profit per worker. CEO Ratio shows you the other side of the same coin: what the man at the top takes home, set beside what his own workers make.

Walk into a store and the app tells you the ratio where you stand.

## The showcase number

**Starbucks: 6,666 to 1.** CEO Brian Niccol's total pay of $97,813,843 against a median Starbucks worker's $14,674 — one man's year equals 6,666 workers' years. That figure comes straight from Starbucks' own SEC filing. They had to publish it by law. This app just makes sure somebody reads it.

## The data rules

- **74 companies**, ratios computed from the companies' own SEC proxy filings (the CEO pay ratio disclosure every public company must file).
- **67 verified ratios; 7 honest blanks** where the filing doesn't support a real number. This app does not guess, estimate, or fill gaps with third-party figures — where the truth isn't on paper, the app shows nothing.
- USD, US-listed companies.

## How it works

Same engine as GroundTruth: one quiet notification while you shop, location-based store detection, and the number for the roof you're standing under. No ads, no accounts, no selling your data.
